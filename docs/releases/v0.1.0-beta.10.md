# AYTHER Runtime v0.1.0-beta.10

This beta fixes the defects found in the spec 002 acceptance campaign of
2026-10-04 in the replay inspection and the replay QA tools.

- A visible inspection that steps back and plays frames again ends with its
  result again; it could end with `runtime_evidence_stream_invalid` and no
  terminal because those frames were counted twice.
- `--profile` without a pack is accepted with the same conditions as the run
  with the pack and is recorded as requested with no effective profile; a
  profile the selected pack does not offer is rejected before the request is
  admitted.
- A take whose initial state is damaged is rejected before the request is
  admitted, in its own field.
- `traversal.toml` records every stretch played between pauses, the origin of
  explicit options equal to their default is `explicit`, and the published
  option inventory names the commit of its sources.
- The cadence of a visible take starts at its first present, so the
  preparation of the first frame no longer degrades its evidence, and the
  debug overlay shows the current phase after a pause or a step.
- Runtime–Play protocol v1, the Engine lock `v0.1.0-rc.15` and the supported
  Engine package range `>=0.1.0,<0.2.0` remain unchanged.

Known: every recovery during an inspection breaks the continuity of the
evidence PCM, so an inspected take reports `pcm_continuity_failed` and its
evidence stays incomplete; a pause without navigation is not affected.

See [Runtime–Engine compatibility](../runtime-engine-compatibility.md) and the
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
