# AYTHER Runtime v0.1.0-beta.11

This beta fixes the defects found in the spec 002 acceptance campaign of
2026-10-05 on `v0.1.0-beta.10`, in the replay QA tools, and the order of pack
overlays in the layer stack (render finding F-1b), and updates the
reproducible Runtime dependency to AYTHER Engine `v0.1.0-rc.16`.

- Pack overlays are drawn at their authored position in the layer stack, as in
  the Lab, instead of on top of everything. An overlay authored behind the
  title, such as clouds between plane B and plane A, is no longer drawn over
  it. Packs baked without overlay positions keep the previous order.

- A finished take no longer loses its evidence when its evidence path reaches
  260 characters. It ended `fact_publication_failed` with no fragments,
  typically the second take of a request with a long destination; the checker
  now writes and reopens the evidence through extended-length paths.
- A request identity longer than 256 bytes, or with a control character, is
  rejected before anything is created, naming `--request-id`
  (`request_id_too_long`, `request_id_invalid`), in Spanish and English.
- A visible take whose presentation was interrupted and recovered (minimized
  window, audio device removed) is no longer accredited as complete: it ends
  `presentation_incomplete`, with the interruption as its reason, like a video
  failure, and its playback still ends naturally.
- The overlay order requires AYTHER Engine `v0.1.0-rc.16`, which exposes each
  overlay's stack position. The release and QA Engine locks move from
  `v0.1.0-rc.15` to the published `v0.1.0-rc.16`; the bootstraps verify its
  SHA-256, the release checksums and its SLSA provenance. Runtime–Play
  protocol v1 and the supported Engine package range `>=0.1.0,<0.2.0` remain
  unchanged.

See [Runtime–Engine compatibility](../runtime-engine-compatibility.md) and the
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
