# AYTHER Runtime v0.1.0-beta.12

This beta brings the replay QA tools to the takes the Lab writes with AYTHER
Engine `v0.1.0-rc.18`, adds pad timelines as replay material (spec 006), and
fixes HD music and input defects of the game session. It updates the
reproducible Runtime dependency to AYTHER Engine `v0.1.0-rc.18`.

- Replay QA accepts `.arp` takes of versions 9 and 10. A v9 take ends with the
  FM register shadow the Engine detector held when the take began; the Runtime
  seeds the live detector with it, so a take that begins with the music
  already playing identifies its instruments and anchors the authored
  sequences as the authoring take did. A v10 take also carries its take id
  (Engine spec 007); it needs the capability `take_format_v10`.
- `ayther_audio_qa check --timeline <file>` drives the replay with a pad
  timeline (`wait 5s`, `press start`, `hold right 0.1s`…) from power-on or
  from a Lab bookmark instead of a recorded take (`timeline_replay_v1`). At
  the natural end, Escape closes the QA window and `--at-end inspect|close`
  chooses whether it stays for the post-end inspection (`close_at_end_v1`).
- The game session decodes the pack's catalog textures before the first HD
  frame and after a hot reload, so the music no longer stutters on scene
  entries while textures are decoded on first use.
- Turning «Modo HD» off and on from the pause menu resumes the HD music at the
  emulated-clock offset, and the button that closes the pause menu no longer
  reaches the game as a new press. The pause menu layout is aligned.
- The release and QA Engine locks move from `v0.1.0-rc.17` to the published
  `v0.1.0-rc.18`; the bootstraps verify its SHA-256, the release checksums and
  its SLSA provenance. Runtime–Play protocol v1 and the supported Engine
  package range `>=0.1.0,<0.2.0` remain unchanged.
