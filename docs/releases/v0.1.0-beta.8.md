# AYTHER Runtime v0.1.0-beta.8

This beta integrates RF-18 music continuity and updates the reproducible
Runtime dependency to AYTHER Engine `v0.1.0-rc.13`.

- Music sequences preserve their identity and position across Runtime state
  transitions, including explicit intro-to-loop progression without restarting
  the intro during title, menu and selection screens.
- Restoration is transactional and drains the previous backend state before
  resuming, with recovery, timing-budget and discontinuity classification tests.
- Reproducible Audio QA evidence records sequence state, audible restart and
  overlap observations, PCM continuity and the semantics consumed by Runtime,
  Lab and Play.
- The Engine lock verifies the four RC.13 packages, published checksums and
  SLSA provenance before configuration.
- Linux quality and sanitizer coverage now includes every Audio QA target.
- TruffleHog is updated from `3.97.2` to `3.97.9` with an immutable SHA pin.
- Runtime–Play protocol v1 and the supported Engine package range
  `>=0.1.0,<0.2.0` remain unchanged.

See [Runtime–Engine compatibility](../runtime-engine-compatibility.md) and the
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
